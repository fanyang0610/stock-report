# Method review, 2026-11-13 (Fable 5.2, read-only)

A fresh-eyes review of the valuation method of both products: the daily screening and the single-stock research
report. Nothing in the code or the data was changed. This file carries the findings and a staged plan to the sessions
that build the fixes. It is a proposal: each stage is adopted when you start its session, and that session writes the
stage into docs/PLAN.md ("Stages" and "Status") as it goes, as every stage before it.

Figures come from the 2026-11-13 snapshots and lists (`data/snapshots/2026-11-13`, `reports/2026-11-13`) and the 25
cached valuations in `data/research/*/*/valuation.json` (built 2026-11-12/13).

## Verdict
- The engineering is sound: point-in-time data, cross-checks, no number from the AI, nothing tuned to the backtest. No
  crucial engineering bug was found.
- Research: the rating mostly follows the reporting currency, and four more faults sit beside that one.
- Screening: the Buy list is in practice an earnings-rebound list, the lists change too fast for a 12-month rating, and
  the Sell rating has no support in the project's own history.
- Your skills: their checks are computed and printed, but none changes a fair value, a band or a rating, and the
  valuation engine breaks three rules of your own investment-research skill.

## Research report: findings

### R1. The discount rate and the cash flows don't match, so the rating follows the currency
- DCF against the price as built. USD reporters: Apple -54%, Amazon -71%, Lilly -76%, Palantir -88%, Snowflake -86%;
  none of the 9 USD names is a Buy. Profitable CNY reporters: 茅台 +39%, 五粮液 +79%, 神华 +44%, 腾讯 +48%, 阿里 +55%.
- Cause. Damodaran's US premium (4.23% mature) is backed out of the S&P 500's price with generous cash flows (five
  years of analysts' growth, then today's payout for ever, which implies a return on equity near 20% for good). The
  model pairs it with conservative ones: base-rate growth after year 2, margins back to the 8-year median, returns
  fading to the cost of capital. For CNY it is the reverse: a 1.6% yield plus ~5% gives 5-7%, far below what the
  market asks (the bank floor of 2026-11-09 patches the same fault: 招商银行's CAPM cost of equity ~5%, the implied
  one 14.8%).
- Check: `dcf.fcff` on a stylized typical firm (base-rate growth, constant margin, beta 1) gives 14-17x after-tax
  operating profit in the US against a market at 23x trailing earnings (17.6x forward), and ~20x in CN against 15x
  (11.7x forward).
- A 2-point move of the rate moves a CNY valuation by 30-50 points (茅台 +39% -> +8%, 腾讯 +48% -> +9%, 神华 +44% ->
  -6%): the terminal value is ~65% of the value at a 5.8% stable cost.

### R2. Margins return fully to the company's own 8-year median within 5 years
- It undoes every recent change, in both directions. Amazon 13.1% today -> 5.6% (holding today's margin: DCF -71% ->
  -48%); Lilly 40% -> 25%. In reverse: 阿里's +55% becomes -49% if today's margin stays; 五粮液 +79% -> +51%.
- PLAN.md ("Amazon's valuation") records that margins above their median "keep little" (US 0.28 after 3 years). I
  could not reproduce it. On `data/history/us_annual.csv.gz` (operating income over revenue, USD filers, gaps of 3+
  points), margins above their median kept a median 78-110% of the gap after 3 years under five window definitions
  (5 or 8 years, with or without the current year), and margins below it recovered fully. Both results are thin
  (2-5 cohorts) and survivors only. The script behind the plan's figure is not in the repo.

### R3. Methods that disagree are averaged with fixed weights
- Amazon: DCF -71%, its own 5-year P/E (40x, from the years it grew far faster) +55%, peers -14%; the report prints
  -24% with a 75% band. TSMC: DCF -7%, own P/E +77%, peers +155%. 恒瑞: -34%, +70%, -5%. Twelve of the 25 ratings carry
  a band of 50% or more and 18 are 持有: the headline fair value is supported by none of its parts.
- Your investment-research skill says the opposite: when methods disagree, explain the economic reason, don't remove
  the disagreement by averaging, and keep it unresolved rather than force a rating.

### R4. Growth and the moat are cut short
- Growth falls to the size class's base rate in year 3 (Lilly 19%, 9.5%, then 7.4%). Fading evenly from the last
  consensus year instead: TSMC -14% -> +36%, NVIDIA +5% -> +33%, 腾讯 +48% -> +61%.
- The moat is all-or-nothing: the return on capital must beat its cost in every one of 8+ years. One weak year
  removes it and cuts the US terminal value to 11x profit (1 / 9.1%). On the median instead: Novo +5% -> +41%.
- The fixed "r - g at least 5 points" comes from `tools/terminal_value.py`, which the skill now calls a historical
  constraint, not a rule for every company. The same tool uses a judged long-run return on new capital (15-40%).
- Morningstar's scale is borrowed for the rating, but not its core: the length of the excess-return period follows
  the moat.

### R5. Mixed earnings bases
- US consensus EPS is adjusted (Zacks, via Nasdaq) while the trailing base is GAAP, so the consensus years' margins
  jump: Lilly 54% and 60%, NVIDIA 79% in year 2, then down to the target. It also adds stock pay back for two years
  in a model that counts it as a cost.
- A 20-F filer is valued off its last fiscal year: TSMC's P/E is 41.6 in research and 27.4 in the screening on the
  same day, and its "year 1" is a year that has nearly ended.
- CN and HK operating profit is pretax profit plus interest, so it counts investment gains and interest income while
  cash is added again (fixed for US filers on 2026-11-12). Tencent's investment portfolio is not in its value;
  `dcf.equity_value` has a `nonop` argument nothing feeds. Not checked: whether Eastmoney's tables carry 投资收益,
  公允价值变动收益, 利息收入 and 长期股权投资 for every company.

### R6. What survives every change
- With today's margins half kept, even growth fade, the moat on the median and the rate moved 2 points, Apple is still
  -47%, Palantir -76%, Lilly -60%. That is a real view (Apple trades at 39x earnings with ~7% growth; the street's own
  target is 4% below its price), to be labelled as one.
- The street is not a neutral yardstick: Amazon 41 buys and 0 sells, NVIDIA 33 and 0, Snowflake 31 and 0; its targets
  for NVIDIA and Micron sit 94-100% above the price. Our 4 Buys all agree with it.

## Screening: findings

### S1. The Buy list is an earnings-rebound list
- Buys' median year-on-year profit growth: CN +63% (market +9%), US +94% (+18%). Growth is the buys' highest family
  (mean rank 0.86 in CN and the US), value the lowest (US 0.69, HK 0.56). US buys' forward P/E 18.7 against a market
  median of 17.6. Five of the US 20 ride one chip cycle (Micron, Sandisk, Western Digital, TSMC, ASML).
- Cause. One earnings spike lifts three families: E/P, forward E/P and EBIT/EV; profit growth; SUE, revisions and
  price. Growth and momentum are correlated (family ranks: CN 0.54, US 0.24, HK 0.26; CN's SUE and profit growth are
  nearly one number) and value runs against growth (US -0.23, HK -0.21), so equal weights give the pair the vote.
- The research module cites Chan, Karceski and Lakonishok (2003) that profit growth doesn't persist, and rates Micron
  持有 after normalizing. The history's growth spread: CN 0.0%, US -1.5%, HK +5.9% (BACKTEST.md section 23).

### S2. The lists change too fast for a 12-month rating
- From 2026-11-07 to 11-13: US Buy kept 16 of 20, Hold 5 of 10, Sell 6 of 10; HK Buy 16, Hold 6; CN Buy 18.

### S3. Sell has no support in the history
- Sell beat the fund on average (CN +3.0%, US +1.5%; section 24). The bottom of a score with momentum and growth picks
  washed-out names that rebound (losers' rebounds: Daniel and Moskowitz 2016). The distress and accruals figures are
  computed (`signals.distress`, `sta`) but only block buys.

### S4. The 预期 line credits the picks with the equal-weighting effect
- 20 names over the fund CN +2.2%, US +1.5%; over the neutral basket CN -1.0% (ahead in 36% of windows), US -1.5%
  (43%), HK +6.8% (75%) (section 25). CN's 10 best scores -0.4%, 5 best -4.9% over the fund.

### S5. Smaller
- A stock with fewer components lands in the tails more often (US momentum on one component: 28% in the top tenth
  against 10%; US growth on two: 25%). Buys overall aren't biased (15.8 components against 15.7).
- The accruals screen removes the US #10 and #13 scores on an effect that has faded in large caps (Green, Hand and
  Soliman 2011).

## Your skills against the project
Read in full: `~/ai-berkshire/skills/*.md`, `skills/references/investment-research/valuation.md`, `tools/terminal_value.py`.
- investment-research: the terminal P/E formula, one currency for r and g and the reverse DCF are in. Broken: no
  averaging of conflicting methods (R3); the 5-point rule as a universal (R4); a judged return on new capital (R4).
  Unused: its central output, the 10-year return today's price gives, swept over discount rates with a check that the
  ranking holds. The project computes that return (`reverse()`'s `implied_wacc`) and only prints it. Discrete risks as
  a tail scenario (delisting, VIE) aren't built: bear and bull are the EPS range and one standard deviation of margin.
- quality-screen, investment-checklist, earnings-review's signals, era-alpha, Beneish, Piotroski, Altman: computed in
  `research/checks.py`, printed, never read by `value.py`; `rating.py` reads only the payout and the average yield.
- income-investment: its cash-coverage test and dividend-cut scenarios aren't in the dividend model.
- Business, moat and management: DeepSeek's words only (rule 2). thesis-tracker and thesis-drift: not built.
- `tools/morningstar_fair_value.py`: not used. An independent fair value is a better yardstick than broker targets.
  Not checked: my query was blocked by the permission system; check its terms of use before the project relies on it.

## Proposed stages (one per session; research 13-15 in order, screening 16-17 in order, the two tracks independent)

13. **Research inputs on one basis** (R5). The consensus years' margins on the same earnings basis as the trailing
    figures; a 20-F filer's trailing twelve months from the second source's interim figures, so research and the
    screening show one P/E; CN (and HK where the tables allow) operating profit without investment gains and interest
    income, with the non-operating stakes at book in the equity bridge. Done when: no consensus-year margin above the
    company's 10-year best without a stated reason; TSMC's research and screening P/E agree within 5%; the 25 cached
    companies rebuilt with a before/after table; tests for each parser change. Model: Opus 5.5, high.
14. **Research long-run assumptions** (R2, R4). A committed script that measures margin persistence in all three
    markets (above and below the median apart, windows stated, the survivor limit said) and the reversion share the
    DCF then uses in both directions; a graded moat (how long and how far the return on capital stayed above its cost)
    that sets how long excess returns last; growth beyond the consensus fading without a cliff, still ending at the
    base rate; the 5-point rule replaced by a stated test of the terminal value's sensitivity. Published evidence
    first (Fama and French 2000; Nissim and Penman 2001; Koller's "Valuation" on ROIC persistence; Morningstar's
    moat-linked fade), the script as the check. Done when: each rule has its evidence in PLAN.md; the 25 rebuilt with
    a before/after table and each large move explained by rule, not by name. Model: Opus 5.5, xhigh.
15. **Research rating** (R1, R3, the skills). The rating on the return the price implies against its market's: each
    market's implied return solved with the model's own cash-flow rules on the market's aggregate (Damodaran's implied
    premium method), replacing the bank floor with one rule; one main method per type with the multiples as a
    cross-check, and a stated unresolved state when they conflict; the skills' checks act (a failed quality screen or
    an earnings red flag blocks 买入 or widens the band; the dividend model tests cash coverage); a tail scenario for
    discrete risks; the absolute view kept as one line. Done when: the cap-weighted market comes out at its price by
    construction; the 25 rebuilt, with the share of Buy / Hold / Sell by currency shown; the report's page 1 and the
    street comparison follow. Model: Opus 5.5, xhigh.
16. **Screening score** (S1, S5). Three families: year-on-year profit growth into momentum, 5-year profit growth
    into quality (quality-minus-junk's place for it), revenue growth judged on its evidence; one normalized value
    measure (price over 5-8-year average earnings: Graham; Gray and Carlisle; Anderson and Brooks 2006) from
    `history.YEARS`; missing components pulled to the middle, not skipped. History rerun (sections 23-25), deciding
    nothing. Done when: PLAN.md has each change with its source; the lists before and after on the same snapshots;
    the family correlations and the buys' profit growth against the market's reported. Model: Opus 5.5, xhigh.
17. **Screening lists and words** (S2, S3, S4). A buffer rule (a rated name stays until it falls well below the cut,
    as MSCI's and S&P's factor indexes do); Sell as expensive, low-quality and distressed names instead of the score's
    bottom; the 预期 line saying what the picks added over the neutral basket. Done when: a week of snapshots shows
    the lists' turnover before and after; Sell's history rerun with its survivor caveat. Model: Opus 5.5, high.

Each stage keeps AGENTS.md's rules: one fresh-context stage review on Sonnet, searches on Haiku, a `Release:` trailer,
nothing sent over WeChat without your OK.

## How to run it
- No standing orchestrator. The research stages edit the same files (`research/value.py`, `rating.py`), so they run
  one after another; this file is the plan, and each stage session ends with the next stage's card and its model.
  Stages 13 and 16 touch different files and can run at the same time.
- Fable is for research only: a check of the 25 rebuilt valuations after stage 15, of the lists after stage 17, or
  when a stage's result contradicts this review.

## How the checks were run (to repeat them)
- One assumption at a time: load a company's cached `facts.json`, build the inputs as `rating.build` does without
  saving, wrap `value.paths` (margin, growth) and `value.moat`, call `rating.methods` and `rating.weigh`.
- Family correlations, component counts and the buys' traits: the day's snapshot (`f_*`, `p_*`, `np_growth`) joined to
  `reports/<day>/<market>_lists.csv`.
- Margin persistence: `us_annual.csv.gz`, margin = `op_income / revenue`, gap to the median of the 5 or 8 years before.

## Not verified
- No fix is built or backtested. The typical-firm multiples use stylized margins and reinvestment.
- The 2-point rate move is an illustration, not a calibrated value.
- The 25 valued companies are ones you asked about, not a random sample.
