# Method review, 2026-11-23 (Opus 5.5, read-only)

A fresh look at the valuation method of both reports, with the user's earlier review by another model (Codex) as input:
its good points are taken, the weak ones left. Commit reviewed: 6e059a6 on main. Nothing in the code or data was
changed; the counterfactuals ran on the project's own functions (research/value.dcf_value, fade.revenue_table) against
the saved data of 2026-11-22. Stages 23-28 in PLAN.md carry the fixes; each finding below says which stage.

## Verdict

- The architecture is sound: code makes every number, DeepSeek writes words that are quote-checked, discount rates are
  the market's own implied return under the model's rules, and every ingredient cites published work. Keep it.
- The research forecast treats every company as the median company: growth fades to the size fifth's base rate,
  margins revert to the company's own 8-year median, capital efficiency comes from its own past. That is an honest
  prior for an ordinary company and a systematic error for a company in transition: it undervalues fast growers and
  turnarounds (688795 at 21 CNY against 270.5) and companies whose margins rose for lasting reasons (Amazon 卖出), and it
  gives the report a style tilt (cheap names 买入, growth names 卖出) that comes from the forecast rules, not the company.
- The screening's own history shows no stock-picking edge in CN and the US against a sector-matched basket, the US score
  inverted, and Sell beating the index fund in CN and HK. All three are what a survivor-only history produces; fix the
  history before reading anything into the method from it.

## Research report

### F1. The consensus bridge (bug; stage 23)
- **A turn to profit is discarded.** When the trailing EPS is 0 or negative, year 1 keeps today's loss margin and drops
  the consensus profit (research/value.py:370, `else m0`). 688795 (摩尔线程): trailing EPS −0.51, the first consensus
  window's EPS 1.05 (about a 9% net margin on 5.32bn of revenue); the model's year-1 margin −6.8% (EBIT −0.36bn) and
  year-2 EBIT −0.03bn where the consensus has a profit of about 0.7bn (2027 EPS 1.48 × 470m shares).
- **One consensus year, from a stale source.** A consensus year needs both Yahoo's revenue and the EPS (value.paths).
  Yahoo's CN revenue is thin: for 688795 its 2026 revenue is 3.60bn while the company had reported 3.29bn for nine
  months (1.55bn in Q3 alone, +319% year on year), and it has no 2028 revenue, so the path keeps one consensus year and
  Eastmoney's 2027 and 2028 EPS (1.48, 4.19) go unused.
- **EPS growth moves operating income.** Operating income is the trailing one × consensus EPS ÷ trailing EPS. Buybacks,
  interest, tax and investment gains move EPS without moving operating income, and the two EPS can be on different
  bases: NVIDIA's report of 2026-11-22 fell back to reported trailing EPS (8.00) against an adjusted consensus because
  Nasdaq's actuals had run a quarter past the fact sheet (Codex's point; Damodaran: operating growth from revenue and
  margin, "valquestions/growth.htm").
- **A quarter behind.** NVIDIA's fact sheet of 2026-11-22 ends 2026-07-26 while its next earnings date, 2026-11-17, had
  passed; the filing list held the release (data/research/us/NVDA/facts.json). Between a release and its 10-Q or
  periodic report the figures are stale, and nothing says so.

### F2. Capital efficiency from another source than the margin (bug; stage 23)
- research/value.py:417 takes the company's own 5-year median sales to capital whenever 3 years have positive capital,
  while a young company's long-run margin is mature peers' (value.paths). A startup's capital, built while revenue was
  tiny, then sets its mature capital needs. 688795: own 0.32, industry 1.11; at the peers' 18.1% margin the return on
  new capital is 0.32 × 18.1% × 75% = 4.3% against a 6.8% cost of capital, so faster growth lowers the value and the
  reverse DCF finds no growth, margin or rate that reaches the price (valuation.json `reverse`: all null).
- Damodaran sets a young company's sales to capital from its industry, as its margin (the Ginzu's defaults; "Valuing
  Young, Start-up and Growth Companies", 2009). With F1's first point and the industry's ratio, 688795's DCF goes from
  20 to 38 CNY (everything else as built).

### F3. The median path, not the expected value (method; stage 25)
- Value is convex in growth: compounding makes a firm's value rise faster than linearly in its growth, so a DCF on the
  median path is below the average value across the paths that can happen. For a mature company the paths are close
  and it doesn't matter; for a fast grower or a turnaround it is most of the gap.
- Measured on the CN revenue tables (2009-2025, today's listings): companies in the top two size fifths whose revenue
  grew 50%+ a year over the 3 years before: 453 ten-year windows. Median 10-year revenue multiple 1.87×, mean 4.13×;
  reached 3× 36%, 5× 18%, 10× 8.2%, 12× 6.2%, 20× 3.1%, 30× 1.5%.
- 688795 on those paths (the project's DCF, industry sales to capital, the consensus's year-1 margin, the margin
  reached by year 5): at an 18% margin, value on the median path 33 (6.8% WACC) / 23 (10%), mean over the paths 48 /
  29; at a 27% margin 46 / 32 and 75 / 45. The share of paths worth 270.5 or more: 0-3.3%. So after the fixes the
  stock is still expensive, but the honest output is "the price needs about 12× today's revenue at a 35% margin, or
  30× at peer margins; about 6% and 1.5% of comparable companies got there", not "fair value 21".
- Sources: Damodaran, "Valuing Young, Start-up and Growth Companies" (2009: simulations over the inputs' distributions,
  and the probability of failure applied to the going-concern value); Mauboussin and Callahan, "The Base Rate Book"
  (2016: the distribution of sales growth by starting size); Rappaport and Mauboussin, "Expectations Investing" (the
  price's implied expectations judged against base rates). The project already has the distributions
  (fade.revenue_table, fade.base_rates) and uses only their median.
- Limit: the history is survivors only, which lifts the distribution's right tail; failures belong in it (stage 27
  gives CN delisted names where a free source has them).

### F4. Margins revert to the company's own past median (method, measure first; stage 26)
- An established company's margin reverts to its own 8-year median at the market's measured keep (value.py:397,
  fade.margin_keep). Fama and French (2000) find profitability reverting toward its expected level (fitted across
  firms), not each firm's past median, and stage 14 measured the keep on any gap of 3 points or more without asking
  whether the gap was a one-year spike or a lasting shift.
- Amazon (2026-11-22): margins 2018-2025 5.3, 5.2, 5.9, 5.3, 2.4, 6.4, 10.8, 11.2% (median 5.6%); the path goes from the
  consensus year's 15.5% to 7.8% by year 10; value 141 against a price of 247, 卖出. With the anchor at 8 / 10 / 12%:
  169 / 192 / 215, which would read 持有 at 10-12% (medium uncertainty). One anchor moves the value by half.
- Stage 14 asked the same question of growth (a 1-year against a 3-year excess: about the same share kept); margins
  were not asked. Measure it the same way before choosing.

### F5. Typing and accounting (bugs; stage 24)
- **A cyclical in a loss is typed a loss-maker.** value.kind_of tests the operating loss (value.py:177) before the
  cyclical test, so a cyclical at its trough gets the loss-maker playbook (mature peers' margin by year 5, own sales to
  capital) instead of its normal year. Micron went from $9.7bn of operating income in FY2022 to a $5.7bn loss in FY2023
  (Codex's example; its figures from Micron's release); today China's solar, lithium and chemical makers in losses are
  the live case.
- **An HK "-B" label overrides the accounts.** value.py:49 and rating.flags make every "-B" name pre-revenue (不评级).
  Codex counted 8 "-B" names with positive trailing revenue in the 2026-11-22 snapshot and cited 百奥赛图-B's filing
  (nine months of 2026: RMB 1.403bn revenue, RMB 312m profit); not re-verified here.
- **Other currencies take the US yield.** value.py:116: `rate(RATE_OF.get(cur, "us"))`. TSMC (TWD) and Novo Nordisk
  (DKK) are discounted at a US yield while forecast in their own currency; stable growth takes the same yield, so the
  error sits mostly in the explicit years. Damodaran publishes a risk-free rate per currency each January.
- **Preferred stock leaves the bridge.** The screening's EV (universe.py:125) and the research's equity bridge
  (research/dcf.py:129) leave preferred stock out, while E/P and B/P take it out of common earnings and book.
  Occidental's 10-Q for September 2026 shows $8.287bn of preferred; Codex measured its EBIT/EV going from 12.46% to
  11.01% on the 2026-11-22 snapshot with it.

### Not taken from Codex, or ranked lower
- The street comparison ("fair value against the street's 12-month target"): compare.street already adds a line saying
  sell-side targets assume the gap closes within a year and ours a third of it. Left.
- Bank book growth on the revenue base rate (China Merchants Bank's payout rising to 90%): real, but the value of a bank
  priced near its group's return moves little with it. Left as a note.
- Correlated bear-case inputs (combined as independent): they set the uncertainty level only. Left as a note.
- The "absolute view" line (Damodaran's premium and an industry beta): for CN banks it gives a 5.2% cost of equity and
  China Merchants Bank 163 CNY against a price of 44. It decides nothing; a reader may take it as a second fair value.
  Left as a note for stage 25 (which rewrites what page 1 shows for valuation ranges).

## Screening report

### F6. The history is survivors only (data; stage 27)
- docs/BACKTEST.md section 27 (after stages 17b and 17d): the 20 names over the sector-matched neutral basket CN −1.5,
  US −0.7, HK +3.2 points a year; the US score's top fifth minus its bottom fifth −6.0 points a year and US quality −9.9
  (section 26); Sell over the index fund CN +6.6, US −0.1, HK +5.4.
- Each is what a survivor-only history produces: the volatile, indebted, unprofitable companies that failed are gone,
  so the ones left look like winners (low quality and Sell lifted), and a basket of hundreds of mid caps is flattered
  most (the basket's own lead over the funds, CN +3.2 and US +3.0). Shumway (JF 1997) found US performance delistings
  lost about 30% in the delisting month alone; Shumway and Warther (JF 1999) about 55% on Nasdaq.
- The history decides no method (PLAN.md, "How decisions are made"), but it fills the 预期 lines the reader acts on, and
  today it can't say whether the score picks in CN and the US. A survivor-free CN history is likely feasible from a free
  source that keeps delisted A-shares (Baostock is the one commonly used for this: to verify, and a new dependency needs
  the user's OK); the US frames keep dead SEC filers, but the price history has none of them.
- Until then the report should show the picks against the sector-matched basket beside the picks against the fund.

### F7. Value measures (bug and measurement; stage 28)
- **CN and HK EBIT** are Yahoo's pretax profit plus interest, so investment gains, fair-value changes and other items
  below operating profit are in EBIT/EV; the US was moved to operating income in stage 17c for that reason, and the
  research module already builds CN and HK operating profit from Eastmoney's statements (research/sheet.ebit).
- **E/P on the average profit** (ep8) sets 8 years of total profit against today's market value, so a company that
  doubled its capital or share count since compares old profit with today's company (PLAN.md says so). Damodaran's
  scaled average, the 8-year average return × today's capital ("Investment Valuation", ch. 22, the passage PLAN.md
  already quotes for the research's cyclicals), keeps Graham's normal year and scales it: the 8-year average ROA
  (prof8) × today's total assets, both already in the data.
- **The live tracker** drops an unresolved name and rescales the rest (track.py:179), so a Sell name that goes to zero
  vanishes from its basket (one of ten at zero and nine flat reads 0% instead of −10%). MSCI keeps a suspended
  constituent at its last price; a delisting is settled at what holders received.

### Not taken, or ranked lower
- Through-cycle earnings for cyclicals in the screen (Codex's first change): F7's scaled average does it for every
  company with data already held; a cyclical-only rule would be a second definition of one component of seven.
- Turnover in CN (Liu, Stambaugh and Yuan 2019's CH-4 sentiment factor) is published CN evidence the score lacks; a
  candidate for a later evidence review, not a stage here.
